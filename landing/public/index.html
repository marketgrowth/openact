Two more apps for growing teams

Docs and Advisory need the OpenAct Director plan. You can try both for free on the demo.

Director plan

Docs

A company library of documents on pages the size of Letter, A4 or Legal paper. Edit together live, with a cursor for each person. Add comment threads and @mentions. Use AI writing help. Sort docs into folders, and share a doc with a link or print it to PDF.

Director plan

Advisory

A marketplace of approved advisors in your company. Members book a consult or pay for an offer in advance. Offers are one-time or monthly retainers. Clients pay through Stripe Connect, and the company keeps a fee. A paid engagement opens a private project for the advisor and the client.

One price per company. No seats.

Start free with the open source core. Add a paid plan for Docs and Advisory. Each paid plan has one yearly price per company. The number of people does not change the price.

How is the price set?

Each paid plan has one yearly price per company. The number of people does not change the price. Choose the plan that fits the size of your company.

How do I pay?

On a hosted OpenAct site, a company owner pays on the Plan page. Stripe takes the payment. On your own deploy, MarketGrowth gives you a license key.

What happens without a plan?

Docs and Advisory lock, but their data stays. The apps come back with all their data when the company gets a plan.

Can I try the paid apps first?

Yes. The demo has Docs and Advisory turned on for every company. The demo deletes all data every night.
