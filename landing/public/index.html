All the world's a stage.

OpenAct is an open source framework for business apps on Cloudflare. Use it to build the apps that run your company, with help from AI agents. You do not have to buy them.

Try the demo Read the docs

The demo is free. Sign in with your email. You do not need a password.

Apps for the work you do every day

Each company in OpenAct turns on the apps it needs. All apps use the same people, companies and roles. A person signs in one time and works in every app.

Projects

Plan work on a Kanban board, a Gantt timeline and a calendar. Ask the AI agent to move late tasks or to write a status summary. Changes show live on every open page.

Docs Director

Write on pages the size of paper. Edit together live, add comments and print to PDF. Sort docs into folders, and share a doc with a link.

Advisory Director

List your approved advisors. Clients book a consult or pay for an offer through Stripe. A paid engagement opens a private project for the advisor and the client.

Your own apps

Sign-in, companies, roles, permissions, email and files are ready. Your app adds only its own features. Add apps from the app directory with one command.

Agents that know their limits

The OpenAct agent works for one person. It never does more than that person's role allows.

  • Deletes and changes to people wait for approval.
  • Company owners choose how much each role can give to the agent.
  • OpenAct records each action that the agent does.

Cloudflare from end to end

OpenAct runs on Workers, D1, R2, Durable Objects, Vectorize and Workers AI. It runs in your own Cloudflare account, and you can deploy it with one click.

The core, the UI and the Projects app are open source under the Apache License 2.0. Docs and Advisory are part of the paid OpenAct Director plan.
