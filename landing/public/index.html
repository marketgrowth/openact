How is the price set?

Each paid plan has one yearly price per company. Choose the plan that fits the size of your company.

How do I pay?

On a hosted OpenAct site, a company owner pays on the Plan page with Stripe. On your own deploy, MarketGrowth gives you a license key.

What happens without a plan?

Docs and Advisory lock, but their data stays. The apps come back with all their data when the company gets a plan.

Can I try the paid apps first?

Yes. The demo has Docs and Advisory on for every company. The demo deletes all data every night.
