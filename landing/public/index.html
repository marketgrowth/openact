All the world's a stage.

OpenAct is an open source framework for business apps on Cloudflare. Use it to build the apps that run your company, with help from AI agents. You do not have to buy them.

Try the demo Read the docs

The demo is free. Sign in with your email. You do not need a password.

Open source, with a working app included

OpenAct is open source under the Apache License 2.0. It comes with Projects, a working app for project management. Use Projects as it is, or use it as a model for your own apps.

Build your own apps

Sign-in, companies, roles, permissions, email, files and the app shell are ready. Your app adds only its own features. AI coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex can build an app for you.

Your Cloudflare account, your control

Deploy OpenAct to your own Cloudflare account with one click. You own the code. Your data stays in your own D1 database and R2 bucket. Nobody else holds it.

BYOAI: bring your own AI

App agents use Workers AI on Cloudflare by default, so you need no other API key. Or bring your own model, such as Claude from Anthropic, or OpenAI. A company owner can add the company's own Claude key in Company settings.

What you get for free

  • Many companies in one install. Each company has its own people, projects, logo and AI settings.
  • Roles and permissions. Owners choose what each role can do in each app.
  • Sign-in without passwords. People get a 6-digit code by email.
  • No seat limits. Invite anyone by email.
  • Kanban board. Make your own columns and drag cards between them.
  • Gantt timeline. Drag bars to move tasks. Tasks group by phase.
  • Calendar. See start dates and deadlines, and drag to change them.
  • An AI agent in Projects. Tell it what to do in plain words.
  • Agents that know their limits. The agent never does more than the person's role allows.
  • Approvals. Deletes and changes to people wait for a person to approve them.
  • An agent log. OpenAct records each action that the agent does.
  • Search by words and by meaning. Find "Book the gala hall" when you type "venue".
  • Live updates. Every open page shows changes at once.
  • Secret projects. Only invited people see them. Others can ask for access.
  • File attachments. Attach files up to 25 MB to a task.
  • An app directory. Add an app with one command.
  • Light and dark themes. Or follow the system setting.
  • Known parts. shadcn/ui, Tailwind CSS and the AG-UI agent protocol.

Docs and Advisory

These two apps need the OpenAct Director plan. You can try both for free on the demo.

Docs Director plan required

A company library of documents on pages the size of Letter, A4 or Legal paper. Edit together live, with a cursor for each person. Add comment threads and @mentions. Use AI writing help. Sort docs into folders, and share a doc with a link or print it to PDF.

Advisory Director plan required

A marketplace of approved advisors in your company. Members book a consult or pay for an offer in advance. Offers are one-time or monthly retainers. Clients pay through Stripe Connect, and the company keeps a fee. A paid engagement opens a private project for the advisor and the client.

Plans and pricing

Start free with the open source core. Add a paid plan for Docs and Advisory. Each paid plan has one annual price per company, with no charge per person.

How is the price set?

Each paid plan has one annual price per company. The number of people does not change the price. Choose the plan that fits the size of your company.

How do I pay?

On a hosted OpenAct site, a company owner pays on the Plan page. Stripe takes the payment. On your own deploy, MarketGrowth gives you a license key.

What happens without a plan?

Docs and Advisory lock, but their data stays. The apps come back with all their data when the company gets a plan.

Can I try the paid apps first?

Yes. The demo has Docs and Advisory turned on for every company. The demo deletes all data every night.
