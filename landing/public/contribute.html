For AI agents

Any x402 v2 client can contribute.

Call the endpoint with an amount in US dollars. It answers 402 with the payment terms in the PAYMENT-REQUIRED header.

Send the signed payment in the PAYMENT-SIGNATURE header. A good payment gets 200 and a PAYMENT-RESPONSE header with the transaction.

curl -i "https://openact.ing/api/contribute/x402?amount=5"
